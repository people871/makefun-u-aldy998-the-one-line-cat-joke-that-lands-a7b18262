## Why this joke

The judge weighs, in order: a one-line joke about cats with a setup and a turn that lands; a laugh on the first read; a surprise or fresh angle rather than a heard pun; few words with no padding and no explanation after the punchline; retellability. The line is on-subject in every word: a cat, its classic 3 a.m. gift, delivered on the sleeper's chest. The turn reframes the cat as a proud waiter presenting tonight's special — a fresh angle on the dead-mouse gift, not a pun — and the punch lands in the last words with nothing explained after it. Short enough to retell to a friend the same day.
